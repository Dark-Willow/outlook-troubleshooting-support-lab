## What I Learned

In this lab, I learned how to document Outlook support cases in a Service Desk format.

I also learned how different Outlook issues can be investigated using Microsoft 365 Admin Centre, Outlook on the web, mailbox settings, shared mailbox settings, forwarding settings, automatic replies, and user account checks.

The final workflow helped organise the troubleshooting process from user report to resolution or escalation.

## IT Support Relevance

Outlook support is a common part of first-line IT Support and Service Desk work.

Technicians need to document user issues clearly, check mailbox and account settings, record troubleshooting steps, and escalate when needed.
